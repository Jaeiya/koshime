# Watch Emphasis

When a user looks at their watch list, all the items will be a certain color. 
That color should indicate how *new* or *old* an item is, since they last
saw it in the list. So for instance, we'll say: 

- `color 1` is for items that have been seen in the last 3 hours
- `color 2` is for items that have been seen within 24 hours
- `no color` or a `neutral color` is for items that have been there longer than 24 hours

This allows the user to know if an anime has just downloaded and 
whether or not they have actually seen it in the list yet.
